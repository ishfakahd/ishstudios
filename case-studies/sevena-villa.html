Creative and digital project work

Sevena Villa is a hospitality project based in Weligama, Sri Lanka. Ish Studios has supported its digital presence through website work and booking-related functionality, alongside digital and content support.

The approach

The project scope has included a public-facing villa website and a dedicated booking journey. The booking workflow has been developed to support guest enquiries and reservation operations. Specific performance outcomes are not published here.

Scope of work

Website design and development; booking platform integration and workflow improvements; social media and content support where commissioned.

The public website is available at sevenavilla.com. Screenshots, approved photography and client-approved outcome metrics should be added to complete this case study.
